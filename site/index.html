What you get

One library

Import a folder, a zip or 7z file, or scan a whole drive. PS3, Wii U, Wii and GameCube games sit side by side, and each starts in its own emulator.

Emulators installed and updated

Omoio installs the official builds of RPCS3, Cemu and Dolphin. RPCS3 is kept up to date, and Cemu and Dolphin are updated to the newest version Omoio has tested, so your controllers keep working. Patches and graphic packs from RPCS3's and Cemu's communities download from the same place, and the patches and cheat codes that come with Dolphin are there to switch on.

Wii and GameCube games also get the graphics mods Dolphin comes with, switched on per game. In Skylanders: Spyro's Adventure and Giants that's Bloom Removal, Bloom Blurred and Native Resolution Bloom.

Controllers and Big Picture

Set one layout for up to four players. In a Wii game your pad is a Wii Remote with a Nunchuk. Big Picture is the library for a TV and a controller, and View and Menu together take you between it and a running game.

Saves and updates

Back up the saves of PS3, Wii U, Wii and GameCube games, and put an older copy back. PS3 games also get their official updates, with the saves backed up before each one.

Skylanders without the portal

In Skylanders Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force and Trap Team on PS3, in SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers and Imaginators on Wii U, and in Spyro's Adventure on Wii, press your controller's home button and a portal menu opens over the game. Pick any figure, trap, vehicle or crystal and it goes on the portal. In Trap Team it also shows which villains you've caught and which trap holds each. In SuperChargers a garage keeps the vehicles by land, sea and sky, and one press puts a SuperCharger on with its own vehicle. In Imaginators your crystals stay together, each new Imaginator gets a crystal of its own, and the Senseis are sorted by battle class.

Questions

Is Omoio an emulator?

No. Omoio is the library and launcher around the emulators. It installs RPCS3 for PS3 games, Cemu for Wii U games and Dolphin for Wii and GameCube games. It starts them for you, and they do the emulation. RPCS3 is kept up to date, and Cemu and Dolphin are updated to the newest version Omoio has tested.

Does Omoio come with games?

No. Omoio plays dumps of games you own. It doesn't include games, download them or help you find them.

Is it free?

Yes. Omoio is free and open source under the MIT licence, and so are the emulators it installs.

What does it run on?

64-bit Windows. PS3 games run through RPCS3, Wii U games through Cemu, and Wii and GameCube games through Dolphin. More systems are planned. How well a game runs depends on the emulator and your PC.

Can I use my controller?

Xbox controllers and other XInput pads work in all three emulators, and so do PS5 and PS4 controllers, without DS4Windows, and the Switch Pro Controller. An 8BitDo pad works in its XInput mode, and in RPCS3 and Cemu in its Switch mode too. Other pads work in RPCS3 through SDL. You set one layout, for up to four players, and in a Wii game your pad is a Wii Remote with a Nunchuk.

What do Wii U games need?

Unpacked game folders and .wua files (Cemu's own archive) work as they are. Disc images (.wud and .wux) need your own keys file, added on the Emulators screen. Downloads in NUS form aren't supported.

What do Wii and GameCube games need?

Dolphin, which Omoio installs. Disc images (.iso, .gcm, .wbfs, .rvz, .wia, .gcz, .ciso and .tgc) play where they are, and so do a disc's files unpacked into a folder. Wii channels (.wad) aren't supported, since a channel is installed into the console rather than played from a disc.

Which Skylanders games work?

On PS3, Skylanders Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force and Trap Team. On Wii U, SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers and Imaginators. On Wii, Spyro's Adventure. Each one gets a portal menu that puts figures, traps, vehicles and crystals on from your controller, so you don't need a real portal. The figures' pictures come from your own copy of the game. SuperChargers and Imaginators on PS3 and Giants, SWAP Force, Trap Team and SuperChargers on Wii don't have the menu yet, and the other Skylanders games aren't supported.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't code signed yet, because a certificate costs money every year. Click More info, then Run anyway. Download it only from this page or from the releases on GitHub.

Something broke. Where do I tell you?

Open an issue on GitHub, ask on Discord, or email hello@omoio.app, with the game, what happened and your Omoio version. The Logs screen keeps a log of every play session. Look it over for personal details before you share it.

Omoio is in early development, and feedback is welcome at hello@omoio.app. The code and the issues are on GitHub, under the MIT licence, you can chat with other players on Discord, and videos are on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Omoio is free and stays free; if you want to help, you can support it on Ko-fi.

Omoio doesn't come with games. You bring dumps of games you own. RPCS3, Cemu and Dolphin are separate projects, and the credit for the emulation is theirs.
