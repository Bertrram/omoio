One layout for every emulator

Set your buttons once, for up to four players, and they work in RPCS3, Cemu and Dolphin. In a Wii game your pad is a Wii Remote with a Nunchuk.

Xbox, PS5 and PS4 controllers and the Switch Pro Controller all work, without DS4Windows. Or play on keyboard and mouse, with the mouse to aim.

Emulators installed for you

Omoio installs the official builds of RPCS3 and Dolphin, and its own build of Cemu 2.6, which adds a portal server. RPCS3 stays up to date, and Cemu and Dolphin move to the newest version Omoio has tested, so your controllers keep working.

For PS3 games it opens Sony's firmware page, and you pick the file you downloaded there.

Saves kept safe

Back up the saves of PS3, Wii U, Wii and GameCube games, and put an older copy back when you need it.

PS3 games also get their official updates, with the saves backed up before each one.

Fixes and mods from each community

Patches and graphic packs from RPCS3's and Cemu's communities download from the same place, and the patches, cheat codes and graphics mods that come with Dolphin are there to switch on.

A fix Omoio turns on by itself says why. Everything else stays off until you turn it on.

Skylanders without the portal

Press your controller's home button, or Insert or Tab on the keyboard, and a portal menu opens over the game. Pick any figure, trap, vehicle or crystal and it goes on the portal, in a moment on PS3 and Wii U, where the menu talks to the emulator's own portal server.

It's there in Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force and Trap Team on PS3, in SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers and Imaginators on Wii U, and in Spyro's Adventure on Wii.

Trap Team shows which villains you've caught, SuperChargers keeps a garage of vehicles, and Imaginators keeps your crystals together.

Omoio doesn't come with the figures' pictures. It reads them from your own copy of the game.

Questions

Is Omoio an emulator?

No. Omoio is the library and launcher around the emulators. It installs RPCS3 for PS3 games, Cemu for Wii U games and Dolphin for Wii and GameCube games. It starts them for you, and they do the emulation. RPCS3 and Dolphin are their official builds, and Cemu is Omoio's own build of Cemu 2.6, which only adds a portal server. RPCS3 is kept up to date, and Cemu and Dolphin are updated to the newest version Omoio has tested.

Does Omoio come with games?

No. Omoio plays dumps of games you own. It doesn't include games, download them or help you find them.

Is it free?

Yes. Omoio is free and open source under the MIT licence, and so are the emulators it installs.

What does it run on?

64-bit Windows. PS3 games run through RPCS3, Wii U games through Cemu, and Wii and GameCube games through Dolphin. More systems are planned. How well a game runs depends on the emulator and your PC.

Can I use my controller?

Xbox controllers and other XInput pads work in all three emulators, and so do PS5 and PS4 controllers, without DS4Windows, and the Switch Pro Controller. An 8BitDo pad works in its XInput mode, and in RPCS3 and Cemu in its Switch mode too. Other pads work in RPCS3 through SDL. You set one layout, for up to four players, and in a Wii game your pad is a Wii Remote with a Nunchuk. Any player can play on keyboard and mouse instead, with every key changeable.

What do Wii U games need?

Unpacked game folders and .wua files (Cemu's own archive) work as they are. Disc images (.wud and .wux) need your own keys file, added on the Emulators screen. Downloads in NUS form aren't supported.

What do Wii and GameCube games need?

Dolphin, which Omoio installs. Disc images (.iso, .gcm, .wbfs, .rvz, .wia, .gcz, .ciso and .tgc) play where they are, and so do a disc's files unpacked into a folder. Wii channels (.wad) aren't supported, since a channel is installed into the console rather than played from a disc.

Which Skylanders games work?

On PS3, Skylanders Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force and Trap Team. On Wii U, SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers and Imaginators. On Wii, Spyro's Adventure. Each one gets a portal menu that puts figures, traps, vehicles and crystals on from your controller, so you don't need a real portal. The figures' pictures come from your own copy of the game. SuperChargers and Imaginators on PS3 and Giants, SWAP Force, Trap Team and SuperChargers on Wii don't have the menu yet, and the other Skylanders games aren't supported.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't code signed yet, because a certificate costs money every year. Click More info, then Run anyway. Download it only from this page or from the releases on GitHub.

Something broke. Where do I tell you?

Open an issue on GitHub, ask on Discord, or email hello@omoio.app, with the game, what happened and your Omoio version. The Logs screen keeps a log of every play session. Look it over for personal details before you share it.
